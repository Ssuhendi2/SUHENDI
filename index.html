About Me

Highly experienced oilfield professional with over 16 years of expertise in Surface Well Testing operations, Data Acquisition, Multiphase Flow Meter Vx (MPFM), and Slickline operations. Proven track record in utilizing advanced technologies such as Multiphase Flow Meter Vx Spectra for both onshore and offshore projects across multiple countries.

Adept at handling high-temperature and high-pressure wells, and proficient in delivering accurate and reliable data. Throughout my career, I have successfully managed complex field projects — from frac flowback operations with 180 stages to offshore MPFM Vx multi-rate tests in unmanned platforms.

I am driven by a commitment to operational excellence, data integrity, and upholding the highest standards of safety (HSE). Beyond field operations, I develop digital tools — including the SWT/DAQ Android app and surfacewelltestguide.com — to bridge the gap between field expertise and digital efficiency.

Core Expertise

Surface Well Testing

Surface Well Testing (SWT), separator operations, ECS (Expendable Choke System), acid flowback, EPF water diversion, and flow rate analysis for both onshore and offshore environments.

Data Acquisition (DAQ)

Real-time DAQ systems management. Sensor installation (Pressure, Temperature, Coriolis), calibration, data quality control (QA/QC), and daily reporting for Shell, Aramco, and national oil companies.

MPFM Vx Spectra

Multiphase Flow Meter Vx operations including PhaseTester, Vx Spectra, multi-rate well tests, maintenance, calibration, and troubleshooting. Experience across Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Brunei, Iran, and Sudan.

Slickline Operations

Well intervention, pressure/temperature surveys, running/pulling plugs and downhole safety valves, shifting sleeves, braided line fishing, and safe pressure control (PCE) operations including H2S environments.
